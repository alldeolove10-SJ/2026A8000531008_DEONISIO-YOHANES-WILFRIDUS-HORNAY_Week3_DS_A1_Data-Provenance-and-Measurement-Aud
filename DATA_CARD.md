## Intended use
Educational data-quality, descriptive-analysis, and basic classification exercises.

## Known limitations
Small historical dataset; limited provenance detail; balanced class counts do not imply natural prevalence; one exact duplicate appears in this frozen representation. Broad ecological, causal, or worldwide-representativeness claims are not supported.

## Intended analysis boundary
This dataset supports descriptive analysis and classification experiments within the frozen dataset. It does not support worldwide population estimates or causal claims about iris species characteristics.
